Why Resurface?

Smart Renovation, Not Demolition

Replacing a tub, shower or countertop means demo, plumbers, tile work and days of mess — often thousands more than your project really needs. Resurfacing restores what you already have.

  • A fraction of the cost. Most customers save 50–70% compared to replacement.
  • Done in 1–2 days. Surfaces are usable again within 24–48 hours.
  • No demolition mess. We protect the work area and leave it clean.
  • Durable factory-grade finish. Professional spray application, not a DIY roller kit.
