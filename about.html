Who We Are

Built on Craft, Not Hype

Resurface Oregon Construction LLC is a locally owned Oregon company focused on one craft: surface restoration. While other contractors juggle a dozen trades, we've dedicated our work to resurfacing porcelain bathtubs, fiberglass showers and countertops — the three upgrades that change how a bathroom or kitchen feels the most, for the least.

Every project is led by a licensed professional (Oregon CCB #248010, licensed, bonded and insured), quoted with a fixed price up front, and finished with the kind of care we'd want in our own homes. If a surface can be restored instead of sent to a landfill, that's better for your wallet — and for Oregon.

  • Locally owned & operated — we live and work in the communities we serve.
  • Licensed, bonded & insured — Oregon Construction Contractors Board #248010.
  • Specialists by design — resurfacing isn't a side service; it's our whole business.
