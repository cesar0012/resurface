Our Services

Resurfacing, Perfected Three Ways

We do one thing — bring worn surfaces back to life — across three specialties: porcelain tubs, fiberglass showers and countertops. Focused craft, fixed pricing, fast results.

Usable again in 24–48h

Service 01

Porcelain Tub Resurfacing

A chipped, stained or dull porcelain tub drags down the whole bathroom. We repair the damage, etch and prepare the original surface, then spray a professional coating that cures into a hard, glossy, factory-smooth finish.

  • Chip & stain repair — damage is filled and blended before refinishing.
  • High-gloss factory-look finish — sprayed, never rolled.
  • Optional anti-slip surface — added texture in the tub floor for safety.
  • Any color change — update almond, harvest gold or faded tubs to bright white.

Repair + Refinish in one visit

Service 02

Fiberglass Shower Repair & Resurfacing

Fiberglass and acrylic showers flex, crack and fade over the years. We repair cracks and soft spots structurally so the damage doesn't come back, then resurface the stall or tub-shower with a durable finish that waterproofs and renews the entire unit.

  • Crack & flex repair — structural fixes, not just cosmetic patches.
  • One-piece & tub-shower units — stalls, surrounds and combos.
  • Wear & UV fading renewed — uniform color wall to wall.
  • Watertight finish — sealed against moisture and everyday cleaners.

Service 03

Countertop Resurfacing

New counters without the demolition. We resurface laminate, tile-edge and worn kitchen or bathroom countertops in modern finishes — solid colors, marble looks or granite looks — so your kitchen or vanity gets a full upgrade in a day or two, at a fraction of replacement cost.

  • Stone-look & solid finishes — samples reviewed at your estimate.
  • Burn, stain & scratch renewal — worn surfaces built back up.
  • Kitchen & bathroom vanities — including backsplash areas.
  • Food-safe once cured — clean with normal household products.
